Notes on file (sheet 07, verbatim)
  • No antidementia medicine (donepezil, galantamine, rivastigmine, memantine) appears on the WHO Model List of Essential Medicines, 24th list (2025). Access to these medicines therefore cannot be tracked as an essential medicines availability indicator; it is tracked through indicators D6 to D9, which follow the Global Dementia Observatory indicator 10 questions on regulatory approval, generic availability and reimbursement.
  • Antiseizure medicines are the tracer medicines named in IGAP for global target 2.2, which is why the basket is built around them.
  • Items T18 to T20 are already on the WHO/HAI global core list, so they are collected in any standard survey at no additional field cost; they are retained here because mid-life hypertension, diabetes and dyslipidaemia are modifiable dementia risk factors under action area 3 of the global dementia action plan.
  • Antipsychotic medicines T14 and T15 are included for availability monitoring only. The mhGAP guideline does not support their routine use for behavioural and psychological symptoms of dementia; the results report should state this explicitly so that availability is not read as an encouragement to prescribe.
  • Any medicine of the global or sub-Saharan Africa core list excluded from a national survey must be reported and justified in the survey report.
